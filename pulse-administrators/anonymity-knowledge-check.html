True or false: what athletes can see in an Anonymous Reporting thread.

1 of 5

The athlete's name is visible in the Anonymous Reporting message thread.

False. The report stays anonymous unless the athlete chooses to include their name in the message text itself.

2 of 5

The name of the administrator who responds appears in the athlete's message thread.

True. The athlete can see which administrator responded, even though their own identity stays hidden.

3 of 5

The athlete can see how many staff members have read their report in the thread.

True. The thread shows the athlete how many staff members have read their report.

4 of 5

An athlete can send multiple follow-up messages in the same thread without revealing their identity.

True. Athletes can continue responding on an active report, and anonymity is maintained throughout.

5 of 5

An athlete's identity is only revealed if they choose to include it in the text of their message.

True. Anonymous Reporting doesn't collect or display the athlete's name — the only way it's shared is if the athlete types it themselves.

Anonymous Reporting protects the athlete's identity by default — administrators only see what the athlete chooses to share.
